How It Works

  1. 1

    Data Cleaning

    2000 student records, 133 missing values imputed with medians, no duplicates.

  2. 2

    Feature Engineering

    One-hot department encoding, ordinal participation, plus 3 engineered features.

  3. 3

    Model Training & Validation

    Stratified 80/20 split, GridSearchCV tuning, 5-fold cross-validation.

  4. 4

    Live Prediction

    Enter student details below and get an instant PASS/FAIL result with risk level.
